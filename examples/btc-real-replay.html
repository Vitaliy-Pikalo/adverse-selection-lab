Decisions are five seconds apart and overlap. The resting bid stays at its original price until the deadline. Only NO-taker trades at that price consume the displayed queue ahead. Cancellations and trades through the bid receive zero queue credit. Unfilled quantity earns zero. Both alternatives exclude fees, latency and market impact. This is a paper execution model.
